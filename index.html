01 About

My work is the intersection of technical skill and analytical thinking.

Two years of computer science at Politehnica University of Bucharest gave me the fundamentals — algorithms, data structures, SQL, Operating Systems, C/C++. A year of sociology at the University of Bucharest added the other half: how to design research, run the statistics, and interpret what the numbers actually mean.

That combination is what I'm most useful for. I can write the query, and also tell you whether the sample it draws from justifies the conclusion someone wants to put in a slide.

I'm based in Germany, expert in English, and actively laerning German. I'm looking for an IT or data-oriented role where both halves get used.
